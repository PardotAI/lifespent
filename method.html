How to study

One rhythm, every week.

Every unit follows the same steps, so your effort goes into the text instead of the format.

Expect 6–10 hours in heavier units (Units 4, 8, 11, 12).

Begin Unit 1 See all units

Step by step

Each step shows a suggested day. It's a pace, not a rule; heavier units may need extra time.

  1. Read

    Day 1

    The primary passages in context. Read the whole chapter, not just the verses.

    Start with the unit's video overview

    Your homework: Notes on the passagesOn each unit page: “Read”

  2. Word study

    Day 2

    Look up the listed Greek and Hebrew terms (STEP Bible, NET notes).

    Safeguard: a word's possible range does not determine its meaning in a passage; context does. Do not argue from an English gloss, from etymology, or from every possible meaning of a lemma.

    Your homework: A short word listOn each unit page: “Word study”

  3. Supplement

    Day 3

    One secondary reading or, in synthesis units, a focused review of earlier material.

    Your homework: Notes from one readingOn each unit page: “Supplement”

  4. Questions

    Day 4

    Answer them in a study journal, including the recurring posture question.

    Your homework: Journal answersOn each unit page: “Questions”

  5. Paper

    Days 5–6

    1–2 pages defending a thesis drawn from the text.

    Draft one day, finish the next

    Your homework: A 1–2 page paperOn each unit page: “Write”

  6. Reflection

    Day 7

    One page answering "What did this show me about God?"

    Your homework: A 1-page reflectionOn each unit page: “Write”
