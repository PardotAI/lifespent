NET translator notes: 2 Timothy 2 · Colossians 4 · 2 Corinthians 10 · Ephesians 6 · 1 Corinthians 13 · Matthew 5

Why these readings

  • 2 Tim 2:14–26Skip the word-fights, handle the word accurately, and correct opponents gently in the hope God grants repentance. Paul states the goal of an argument plainly.
  • Col 4:2–6Prayer first, then wise conduct with outsiders, then gracious speech. The order is the lesson.
  • 2 Cor 10:1–6Paul tears down arguments and takes thoughts captive, and he appeals "by the meekness and gentleness of Christ" in the same paragraph.
  • Eph 6:10–20The armor of God. The fight is spiritual, and Paul ends by asking for boldness to speak.
  • 1 Cor 13Without love, the best argument is noise. You need this before you finish.
  • Matt 5:43–48Love your enemies. The person across the table isn't your enemy, and if they were, this would still apply.
