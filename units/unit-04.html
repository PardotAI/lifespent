Why these readings

  • Isa 40–48God puts the idols on trial and dares them to predict the future. It's the longest courtroom argument in Scripture.
  • 1 Kgs 18:16–40Elijah on Carmel. A public test with rules that both sides accept, and the false god fails.
  • Ps 115Isaiah shows how idols get made and Ps 115 adds what they do to the people who trust them: "those who make them become like them."
  • Jer 10:1–16A craftsman cuts down a tree, decorates it, and nails it in place so it won't tip over. Jeremiah shows that exposing a false belief can be part of a defense.

Other accepted systems exist (e.g., modern Greek, reconstructed Koine), so you may hear these said differently. The author's context decides the meaning.
