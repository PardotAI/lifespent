Why these readings

  • Isa 40–48God puts the idols on trial and dares them to predict the future. It's the longest courtroom argument in Scripture; the three focus passages are where it's sharpest.
  • 1 Kgs 18:16–40Elijah on Carmel. A public test with rules both sides accept, and the false god fails. You'll ask what made it a fair test.
  • Ps 115Isaiah shows how idols get made. Ps 115 adds what they do to the people who trust them: "those who make them become like them."
  • Jer 10:1–16A craftsman cuts down a tree, decorates it, and nails it in place so it won't tip over. Jeremiah shows that exposing a false belief can be part of a defense.
