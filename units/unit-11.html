NET translator notes: Acts 22 · Acts 23 · Acts 24 · Acts 25 · Acts 26

Why these readings

  • Acts 22–26Paul speaks in his own defense in five settings: the Jerusalem crowd, the council, Felix, Festus, and Agrippa. Read them in order. The central claims stay consistent while the details and approach change with each audience.

Say each word aloud and stress the bold syllable. Greek follows the classroom (Erasmian) pronunciation most seminaries teach; Hebrew follows standard academic pronunciation. Other accepted systems exist (e.g., modern Greek, reconstructed Koine), so you may hear these said differently. The author's context decides the meaning.
