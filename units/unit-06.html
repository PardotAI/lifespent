NET translator notes: Luke 1 · John 19 · John 20 · John 21 · 1 John 1 · 2 Peter 1 · Deuteronomy 19

Why these readings

  • Luke 1:1–4The author, traditionally Luke, says he followed everything carefully and drew on what eyewitnesses handed down. It's a historian's statement of method from inside the NT.
  • John 19:35; 20:30–31; 21:24John keeps telling you who saw what and why he wrote it down. Three short passages, one claim: this is testimony, written so you will believe.
  • 1 John 1:1–4"What we have heard, what we have seen with our eyes, and our hands have touched." Witness of the physical, checkable kind.
  • 2 Pet 1:16–21Peter says they didn't follow clever myths; they were eyewitnesses. Then he grounds prophecy in God rather than human will. Both halves matter here.
  • Deut 19:15A matter stands on two or three witnesses. This is the legal standard the NT writers are working with.

Questions

  1. What does Luke claim about his method?
  2. Why does John state his purpose so explicitly?
  3. How does Peter set eyewitness testimony against "cleverly devised myths"?
  4. What does manuscript evidence establish about the wording we have, and how is that a separate question from whether the witnesses are trustworthy?
