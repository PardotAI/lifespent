Read

Read each passage in context: the whole chapter, not just the verses. Links open the NET Bible in the Bible app (bible.com).

Matt 12:22–37 21:23–27 22:15–46 John 5:31–47 8:12–59 10:22–39

Why these readings

  • Matt 12:22–37Accused of working by Satan's power, Jesus shows the charge undercuts itself: a divided kingdom can't stand. A clean reductio.
  • Matt 21:23–27Asked about his authority, Jesus asks about John's baptism. His question exposes why they're asking.
  • Matt 22:15–46Four traps in one chapter: taxes, the resurrection, the greatest commandment, and then Jesus' own question about David's son. Watch him answer, then take the initiative.
  • John 5:31–47Jesus calls his witnesses: John the Baptist, his works, the Father, and the Scriptures. Unit 6's testimony argument, from Jesus himself.
  • John 8:12–59A long, sharp exchange that ends with "before Abraham was, I am." Jesus doesn't soften a claim to win the room.
  • John 10:22–39Jesus argues from Ps 82 and from his works. A short example of reasoning from the other side's own Scripture.
