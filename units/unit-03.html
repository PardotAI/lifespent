NET translator notes: Psalms 19 · Romans 1 · Romans 2 · Acts 14 · Ecclesiastes 3

Why these readings

  • Ps 19Creation speaks in verses 1–6 and the law speaks in verses 7–14. One psalm shows you what nature reveals and what it can't.
  • Rom 1:18–2:16The main text on what everyone knows about God and suppresses. Continue reading further into chapter 2, because the argument about conscience concludes at 2:16.
  • Acts 14:8–18Paul at Lystra, talking to pagans who have no Bible. He argues from rain, harvests, and food. It shows natural revelation in a missionary encounter.
  • Eccl 3:11God "has set eternity in the human heart." It explains why people keep asking questions they can't answer on their own.
