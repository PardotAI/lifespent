Say each word aloud and stress the bold syllable. Greek follows the classroom (Erasmian) pronunciation most US seminaries teach; Hebrew follows standard academic pronunciation. Other accepted systems exist (e.g., modern Greek, reconstructed Koine), so you may hear these said differently. Context, not a word's possible range, decides its meaning.
