NET translator notes: 1 Corinthians 15 · Matthew 28 · John 20 · John 21 · Acts 2 · Acts 17

Why these readings

  • 1 Cor 15The earliest written list of resurrection witnesses, followed by Paul's argument that everything depends on it. Read all of it. Verses 12–19 are your paper.
  • Matt 28The empty tomb, the guards' cover story, and the commission. Matthew answers an objection that was already going around (28:11–15).
  • John 20–21Thomas asks for evidence and gets it. Jesus cooks breakfast on the beach. These physical details rule out a vision or a ghost.
  • Acts 2:22–36Peter's first sermon, seven weeks later, in the city where it happened. He appeals to what his audience already knows.
  • Acts 17:30–31In Athens Paul names the resurrection as God's proof to everyone. It ties the event to the call to repent.
