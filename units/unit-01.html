NET translator notes: 1 Peter 3 · Jude 1 · Philippians 1 · Acts 22 · Acts 25 · 2 Timothy 2 · Colossians 4

Why these readings

  • 1 Pet 3:8–17This is the anchor for our course. Peter tells you to be ready with a defense, but he sets it inside a passage about suffering and a good conscience.
  • Jude 3–4Jude planned to write about salvation and switched to "contend for the faith." Pay particular attention to verse 4: people inside the church were bending the truth. Unfortunately, some of our defense is aimed at insiders, not only skeptics.
  • Phil 1:7, 12–18Paul uses apologia for his own defense of the gospel while in chains. His trial ends up spreading the message.
  • Acts 22:1; 25:16Apologia in a courtroom: a formal answer to charges. Understanding the word's everyday meaning is important before Peter applies it to every believer.
  • 2 Tim 2:24–25How to argue: kind, patient, not quarrelsome, correcting with gentleness. Use these as a lens next to 1 Pet 3:15.
  • Col 4:6One verse on speech "seasoned with salt." It's short on purpose and it's foundational to every unit.
