Why these readings

  • Job 1–2, 38–42The setup and the answer. You see the heavenly scene Job never sees. Then God answers from the whirlwind without disclosing that scene or giving Job the explanation the reader has.
  • HabakkukBabylon is still coming, but Habakkuk moves from complaint toward waiting and worship (3:17–19).
  • Ps 73Why do the wicked do so well? Pay particular attention to verse 17, in the sanctuary.
  • Rom 8:18–39Paul weighs present suffering against future glory and says nothing can separate you from God's love.
  • Gen 50:20"You meant evil against me, but God meant it for good." One event, two intentions.
