A 12-Week Self-Study

Spend your life going deeper in Scripture.

This course explores how beautifully Scripture is woven together and how its truths resound throughout. We study how God, Jesus, and the apostles presented the truth and how you can share it too.

The Flow

  1. Take it in

    • WatchA short video overview sets the scene.
    • ReadThe primary passages, whole chapter in context.

  2. Dig in

    • Word studyThe key Greek and Hebrew terms.
    • SupplementOne focused secondary reading.
    • QuestionsAnswered in your study journal.

  3. Respond

    • Paper1–2 pages defending a thesis from the text.
    • ReflectionWhat did this show me about God?
