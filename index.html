A 12-Week Self-Study

Spend your life going deeper in Scripture.

Scripture speaks for itself. In it, God presents arguments, as do Jesus and the apostles. Each week, you’ll read these arguments in context and reflect on what they mean for you as a believer.

Scripture is the final authority.

This course looks at how Scripture argues for itself by looking at how God, Jesus and the apostles argued for the truth and what that means for you. It's a completely biblical approach: Scripture is the final authority, and all other reading simply supports the text. We follow Paul's example in Acts 17:28 where he quotes a line from Aratus to serve the gospel.

Workload: expect 6–10 hours in heavier units (Units 4, 8, 11, 12).

Every week

The same six steps every week for each unit.

1

Read

The primary passages in context. Read the whole chapter, not just the verses.

2

Word study

Look up the listed Greek and Hebrew terms (STEP Bible, NET notes).

Safeguard: a word's possible range does not determine its meaning in a passage; context does. Do not argue from an English gloss, from etymology, or from every possible meaning of a lemma.

3

Supplement

One secondary reading or, in synthesis units, a focused review of earlier material.

4

Questions

Answer them in a study journal, including the recurring posture question.

5

Paper

1–2 pages defending a thesis drawn from the text.

6

Reflection

One page answering "What did this show me about God?"
