Purpose

To understand how Scripture defends itself, how God, Jesus and the apostles argued for the truth, and to grow in faith through rigorous study. The approach is 100% Biblical: Scripture is the final authority, and supplemental readings are used only as they serve the text (as Paul used Aratus in Acts 17:28).

Pace & workload

Self-paced, about one unit per week. Expect expect 6–10 hours in heavier units (Units 4, 8, 11, 12).

The weekly rhythm

Every unit follows the same six steps. Read the full method →

1. Read
the primary passages in context. Read the whole chapter, not just the verses.
2. Word study
look up the listed Greek and Hebrew terms (STEP Bible, NET notes).
Safeguard: a word's possible range does not determine its meaning in a passage; context does. Do not argue from an English gloss, from etymology, or from every possible meaning of a lemma.
3. Supplement
one secondary reading or, in synthesis units, a focused review of earlier material.
4. Questions
answer them in a study journal, including the recurring posture question.
5. Paper
1–2 pages defending a thesis drawn from the text.
6. Reflection
one page answering "What did this show me about God?"
